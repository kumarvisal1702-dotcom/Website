About

I AM

A student of history who got tired of watching the present get misread.

I'm Kumar Vishal — based out of Jaipur, originally from Madhepura, Bihar. I'm currently pursuing History Honours, and if there's one thing studying the past teaches you, it's that most “breaking news” isn't actually new — it's an old pattern wearing a new headline. That's the lens I bring to this channel.

I make videos in Hindi because the stories that shape our politics, our society, our everyday decisions deserve to reach people in the language they actually think in — not diluted, not oversimplified, just made clear. No borrowed outrage, no manufactured urgency. Just context, explained properly, so you can form your own opinion instead of inheriting someone else's.

This channel is still growing, and so am I — one video, one story, one honest explanation at a time.
